Our Services

Carrollton's Complete Plumbing Solutions

From routine maintenance to emergency repairs, Carrollton Plumbing Service handles every plumbing need for homes and businesses.

Drain Cleaning

Hydro-jetting and professional drain snaking for kitchen, bathroom, and main line drain clogs. Fast and complete clearing guaranteed.

Water Heater Service

Tank and tankless water heater installation, repair, and full replacement. Same-day hot water restoration available.

Leak Detection & Repair

Find hidden pipe leaks with advanced technology before they cause damage. Accurate diagnosis and lasting repairs.

Fixture Installation

Toilets, faucets, sinks, and shower systems installed by experienced plumbers. Full bathroom and kitchen plumbing upgrades available.

Sewer Line Service

Camera inspections, root intrusion removal, and complete sewer line replacement. We diagnose before we dig — every time.

Emergency Plumbing

24/7 response to burst pipes, major leaks, and sewage backup. We answer when other companies do not.
