What We Treat

Comprehensive Pest Solutions

From one-time treatments to year-round protection plans — IntelliPEST covers every pest problem homes and businesses in Grapevine face.

Cockroach Elimination

German, American, and Oriental cockroaches require different treatment protocols. We identify the species and apply targeted, residual treatments for complete elimination.

Rodent Control

Mice and rats in Grapevine homes cause real damage. We seal entry points, set strategic traps, and monitor activity until your home is completely rodent-free.

Termite Treatment

North Texas homes face constant termite pressure. We use both liquid soil treatments and bait station systems to create a perimeter no colony can breach.

Mosquito Control

Outdoor living in Grapevine shouldn't mean swatting all season. Our barrier spray program keeps your yard mosquito-free from spring through fall.

Bed Bug Treatment

Heat treatment and targeted chemical application eliminate bed bugs at every life stage. We don't leave until every last one is gone — no callbacks needed.

Ants & Stinging Pests

Fire ants, carpenter ants, wasps, and hornets. We treat the source — not just the surface — so colonies don't rebuild after the spray dries.
