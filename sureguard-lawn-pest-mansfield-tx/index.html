Lawn & Pest Protection

Complete Lawn & Pest Solutions

Sureguard is the only company in Mansfield that handles your lawn and your pest control under one roof — one call, one relationship, one bill.

Lawn Fertilization & Weed Control

Customized seasonal fertilization programs paired with pre- and post-emergent weed control that transforms patchy, struggling lawns into lush, thick turf within a single growing season.

General Pest Control

Comprehensive interior and exterior pest treatments targeting ants, roaches, spiders, silverfish, and other common household invaders. Quarterly maintenance keeps your home protected year-round.

Termite Protection

Licensed termite inspection, liquid barrier treatment, and ongoing bait station monitoring to protect your home's structure from subterranean termite damage — the most costly pest problem in Texas.

Mosquito Barrier Treatment

Targeted spray treatments applied to shrubs, trees, and low-lying vegetation dramatically reduce mosquito populations through the spring and summer months so you can actually enjoy your outdoor space.

Fire Ant Control

Two-step fire ant elimination using broadcast bait treatments followed by individual mound applications. We eradicate existing colonies and create a barrier that prevents new ones from establishing on your property.

Lawn Disease & Fungus Treatment

Diagnosis and treatment of brown patch, take-all root rot, dollar spot, and other turf diseases that can devastate a lawn. We identify the pathogen and apply targeted fungicide programs that restore turf health.
