Our Services

Complete Termite & Pest Defense

From termite colonies to rodent invasions — Knight's handles the full range of pest threats facing La Porte and east Houston homes.

Termite Treatment

Liquid barrier treatments and bait station monitoring designed to eliminate existing colonies and prevent new infestations from forming around your foundation.

General Pest Control

Roaches, ants, spiders, silverfish — quarterly and bi-monthly programs that keep your home protected through every season without disrupting your household.

Mosquito Control

Yard barrier spray treatments targeting mosquito resting and breeding sites. Monthly applications from spring through fall so your outdoor spaces stay usable.

Rodent Exclusion

Entry-point inspection and sealing, bait station placement, and trapping programs. We keep mice and rats out of your walls and attic before they cause real damage.

Bed Bug Treatment

Full-room inspection and treatment protocols for complete bed bug elimination. We inspect mattresses, furniture, and baseboards — not just the obvious spots.

Scheduled Protection Plans

Monthly, bi-monthly, and quarterly service plans that keep every seasonal pest threat handled proactively — so you never wake up to a surprise infestation.
