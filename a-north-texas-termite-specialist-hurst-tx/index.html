What We Do — And Only What We Do

Termite Control. Fully Specialized.

We have spent over 20 years mastering a single discipline. Every service we offer exists to find, eliminate, and prevent termites — nothing more, nothing less.

Subterranean Termite Treatment

The most destructive termite species in North Texas. We use liquid barrier and baiting system approaches to eliminate underground colonies and cut off access to your structure at the foundation level.

Drywood Termite Control

Drywood termites infest wood directly without soil contact, making them harder to detect and eliminate. Our targeted treatment methods reach colonies inside wall voids, attic framing, and furniture.

Termite Inspection & Report

Comprehensive inspections with detailed written reports identifying every area of activity, risk, and prior damage. Used for real estate transactions, peace of mind, and establishing a treatment baseline.

Termite Bait Stations

In-ground bait station systems placed around your home's perimeter intercept foraging termites before they reach your structure. A low-disruption, ongoing protection approach ideal for active colonies and prevention.

Fumigation Services

Whole-structure fumigation for severe drywood termite infestations. We manage the entire process from pre-treatment prep to post-fumigation clearance, ensuring complete colony elimination throughout the structure.

Annual Termite Monitoring

Year-round monitoring programs keep a specialist's eyes on your property. We inspect bait stations, check for new activity, and intervene immediately if any termite pressure is detected — before damage occurs.

Our Process

Precision from First Call to Final Clearance

Every engagement follows a disciplined protocol. We identify precisely, treat methodically, and monitor continuously.

1
Thorough Inspection

We inspect the entire structure — foundation, crawl spaces, attic framing, wood-to-soil contact points, and moisture areas. We map every area of concern and document findings in a detailed written report before any treatment is discussed.

2
Tailored Treatment Plan

No two infestations are identical. Based on species, colony location, and structural conditions, we design a treatment plan using the most effective method for your specific situation. We explain every recommendation before we proceed.

3
Treatment and Monitoring

Precision treatment is followed by scheduled monitoring to confirm colony elimination and catch any new activity early. Annual monitoring plans keep a specialist actively watching your property year-round.
