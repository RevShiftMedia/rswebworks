Our Programs

Complete Lawn Care Solutions

Weedex offers a full suite of science-backed lawn treatments designed for Texas lawns — keeping them green, healthy, and weed-free all year long.

Lawn Fertilization Program

A structured multi-application fertilization plan tailored to Texas soil and climate, delivering the nutrients your lawn needs to thrive season after season.

Weed Control & Prevention

Targeted broadleaf weed elimination combined with ongoing prevention to stop weeds from taking hold and stealing nutrients from your grass.

Grub Control

Protective grub treatments applied at the right time of year to prevent larvae from destroying your lawn's root system before damage becomes visible.

Lawn Disease Treatment

Expert diagnosis and treatment of common Texas lawn diseases including brown patch, dollar spot, and take-all root rot before they spread and cause lasting harm.

Aeration & Overseeding

Core aeration breaks up compaction so water, air, and nutrients reach the roots. Paired with overseeding to fill thin spots and build a denser, healthier turf.

Pre-Emergent Weed Prevention

Timely pre-emergent applications create a protective barrier in the soil, preventing crabgrass, goosegrass, and other annual weeds from germinating in your lawn.
