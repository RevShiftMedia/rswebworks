Our Services

Complete Plumbing — DFW Metroplex Wide

All Metroplex Plumbing handles every residential and commercial plumbing need across Carrollton, Lewisville, and the entire DFW area.

Drain Cleaning

Professional hydro-jetting and drain snaking to clear any clog fast. Kitchen, bath, and main sewer line service throughout the metroplex.

Water Heater Service

Tank and tankless water heater installation, repair, and replacement. Same-day hot water restoration for urgent situations.

Leak Detection & Repair

Advanced technology to find and fix hidden leaks in walls, slabs, and underground pipes before they become major damage.
