What We Do

Full-Service Plumbing
for Euless Homes & Businesses

From dripping faucets to full repipes, Woodie Woods handles it all — backed by proper licensing, real warranties, and work you can count on.

Leak Detection & Repair

Pinpoint and fix leaks fast before they become costly water damage. Slab leaks, pipe leaks, and hidden leaks all handled with precision.

Drain Cleaning

Slow or blocked drains cleared quickly and completely. Kitchen, bath, floor drains — we restore proper flow without damaging your pipes.

Water Heater Services

Tank and tankless water heater repair, replacement, and new installation. Get reliable hot water back fast — most units installed same day.

Fixture Installation

Faucets, sinks, toilets, showers, and tubs — installed right the first time. We handle all brands and styles for kitchens and bathrooms.

Whole-Home Repipe

Aging galvanized or polybutylene pipes replaced with modern PEX or copper. Better water pressure, cleaner water, and lasting peace of mind.

Emergency Plumbing

Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewage backups — when time matters, Woodie Woods responds. 24/7 emergency service for Euless and surrounding areas.
