What We Treat

Expert Solutions for Every Pest

From one-time treatments to ongoing protection plans — we handle the full range of pests that Flower Mound homeowners face throughout the year.

General Pest Control

Ants, roaches, silverfish, earwigs, and seasonal invaders treated with targeted, residual applications that keep your home protected year-round.

Rodent Control

Full exclusion work — we find every entry point, seal it, and eliminate the active infestation. No more mice or rats returning through the same old gaps.

Termite Protection

Liquid barrier treatments and bait station monitoring protect your home from subterranean termites — the leading cause of structural damage in North Texas.

Mosquito & Tick Control

Seasonal barrier spray programs that let you enjoy your yard again. Applied to shrubs, trees, and shaded areas where mosquitoes and ticks harbor.

Bed Bug Elimination

Comprehensive heat and chemical treatments targeting every life stage. We don't leave until the infestation is fully eliminated and documented.

Wasps & Stinging Insects

Safe removal of yellow jackets, hornets, and paper wasps from eaves, attics, and ground nests — without putting your family at risk.
