What We Do

Plumbing Services in Pasadena

From slow drains to emergency leaks, Starkey Plumbing Co handles it all with the care your home deserves.

Drain Cleaning

Stubborn clogs, slow drains, and backed-up lines cleared fast. We use professional-grade equipment to get your drains flowing again.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water is never acceptable. We diagnose and repair water heaters fast — or replace them the same day when needed.

Pipe Repair

Leaking or burst pipes can cause serious damage quickly. Our crew locates the problem and makes durable repairs that hold.

Fixture Install

Faucets, sinks, showers, and toilets installed properly by a licensed plumber. No shortcuts, no leaks.

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks waste water and destroy walls. We use proven detection methods to find the source without unnecessary damage.

Emergency Service

Plumbing emergencies don't keep business hours. Call us any time — we pick up and we show up, day or night.
