Our Programs

How We Serve Pasadena's Animals

From adoption and fostering to lost pet recovery — we connect animals with the people and care they need.

Pet Adoption

Dogs, cats, rabbits, and small animals — all vaccinated, microchipped, and spayed or neutered before going home. Affordable adoption fees make it easy to say yes.

Foster Program

Fostering gives animals a safe, loving home environment while they wait to be adopted. We provide food, supplies, and vet care — you provide the warmth and attention.

Volunteer Opportunities

Dog walkers, cat socializers, event helpers, and administrative volunteers all needed. No experience required — just a love of animals and a few hours a week.

Lost & Found Pets

Lost your pet or found a stray? We maintain a lost-and-found registry for Pasadena area animals. Bring found animals to our shelter — we'll help reunite them with their families.

Donations & Supplies

We gratefully accept food, bedding, toys, leashes, and monetary donations. Every contribution directly supports the animals in our care as they wait for their forever families.

Owner Surrender Intake

Sometimes life circumstances change. We accept owner surrenders with compassion and no judgment — ensuring your pet receives proper care and finds a loving new home.

Why Adopt Here

Why Adopt from Pasadena Animal Shelter

Adopting from a shelter saves a life. Here's everything that comes with your new family member.

Vaccinated & Microchipped

Every adoptable animal leaves our shelter fully vaccinated and microchipped, giving them a safe, traceable start to their new life.

Spayed or Neutered

All adopted pets are spayed or neutered before placement — so you can focus on bonding with your new companion, not unexpected litters.

Affordable Adoption Fees

Our fees are kept low so that cost is never a barrier between an animal and a loving home. Everything included in the fee would cost far more at a vet or breeder.

Knowledgeable Staff

Our team gets to know every animal in our care — their personality, energy level, and ideal home environment — so we can help match you with the right pet.

4-Star Community Rating

823 Google reviews from Pasadena families who've adopted, volunteered, and brought animals to us over the years. We're proud of what those reviews represent.

You Save a Life

Shelter adoption directly saves the life of the animal you bring home — and creates space to help another animal in need. There is no more meaningful way to get a pet.

Adoption Process

How Adoption Works

Three simple steps to bring your new best friend home from Pasadena Animal Shelter.

01

Visit & Meet

Come in during open hours and spend time with our available animals. Our staff will introduce you to pets that match your lifestyle, living situation, and family needs.

02

Apply & Approve

Complete a brief adoption application. We review it quickly — our goal is to match animals with loving homes, not create unnecessary barriers. Same-day approvals are common.

03

Bring Them Home

Pay the adoption fee, receive all records, and take your new family member home. Our team is always available for follow-up questions as you get settled in together.
