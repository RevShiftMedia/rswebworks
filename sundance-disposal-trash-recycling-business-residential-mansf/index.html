Our Services

Complete Disposal Solutions

Whether you need a weekly pickup schedule or a one-time cleanout, Sundance Disposal has the right solution for your home or business in Mansfield and surrounding areas.

Residential Trash Pickup

Reliable weekly and bi-weekly curbside pickup for single-family homes and residential communities. Never miss a collection day again.

Recycling Services

Comprehensive curbside recycling collection for paper, cardboard, glass, plastics, and metals. We help you keep materials out of the landfill.

Bulk Item Pickup

Scheduled pickup for furniture, appliances, mattresses, and oversized items. We haul away what won't fit in your regular bin, quickly and affordably.

Commercial Waste

Dependable waste services for restaurants, offices, retail stores, and multi-family properties. Flexible pickup schedules and competitive commercial rates.

Roll-Off Dumpster Rental

10, 15, 20, and 30-yard roll-off containers for home renovations, roofing projects, estate cleanouts, and construction. Delivered and picked up on your schedule.

Green & Yard Waste

Seasonal pickup for grass clippings, branches, leaves, and landscaping debris. Composted or mulched responsibly — keeping your yard and our county green.
