Our Process

How mender Works

Our four-step ITAD process ensures your data is protected, your assets are handled responsibly, and you receive complete documentation.

1

Asset Intake & Inventory

We catalog every device by serial number, condition, and model. Your complete asset manifest is generated at intake.

2

Secure Data Destruction

All storage media wiped to NIST 800-88 standards. Physical destruction available for drives requiring shredding.

3

Sort, Grade & Recover

Functional assets graded for refurbishment or resale. Non-functional equipment sorted for certified downstream recycling.

4

Documentation & Close-Out

Certificates of Destruction, chain-of-custody records, and a final disposition report delivered to your team.
