Services

Comprehensive Pest Management

From general pest prevention to targeted specialty treatments, every Ephlora service is built on thorough inspection and proven methodology.

General Pest Control

Year-round barrier programs targeting ants, roaches, spiders, earwigs, and seasonal pests. Interior and exterior treatments on a consistent schedule.

Termite Solutions

Targeted liquid treatment systems and baiting programs to protect your home's structural integrity. Detailed inspections document all activity before treatment begins.

Mosquito Control

Barrier spray programs applied to vegetation and resting sites that eliminate 90%+ of active mosquitoes. Seasonal programs and one-time event sprays available.

Rodent Exclusion

Thorough entry-point identification, structural sealing, and trapping systems. We stop rodents at the source — not just manage an ongoing infestation.

Bed Bug Treatment

Multi-stage elimination using heat treatment and targeted chemical applications. All life stages addressed — eggs through adults — with a re-treatment guarantee.

Specialty Pest Solutions

Wasps, scorpions, fire ants, fleas, ticks, and other specialty pests handled with targeted protocols. We identify the specific pest and apply the most effective solution.
