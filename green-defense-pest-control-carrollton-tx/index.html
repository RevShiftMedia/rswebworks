Our Services

Targeted Defense for Every Threat

We identify the specific pest, find where it's coming from, and eliminate it at the source — not just the surface.

Roach Control

German roaches hide in kitchen voids and breed faster than most treatments can keep up. We use residual baits in harborage areas to collapse the colony — not just knock back the population.

Free Estimate →
Rodent Control

Roof rats are active in the Carrollton tree canopy; mice follow the cold inside every fall. We seal entry points and clear the infestation before they establish in your walls.

Free Estimate →
Termite Treatment

Subterranean termites travel underground and can consume a home's structural wood for years before detection. Our baiting and liquid barrier systems stop them before costs climb.

Free Inspection →
Mosquito Control

Josey Ranch Lake and Carrollton's greenbelt corridors breed heavy mosquito populations through summer. We treat foliage and standing water sources to give you your yard back.

Free Estimate →
Fire Ant & Ant Control

North Texas clay soil and warm winters let fire ant colonies grow enormous. We treat mound systems and broadcast granulars to eliminate colonies and maintain a barrier around your property.
