Our Services

All-Season Pest Protection

Every pest problem, every time of year. Our team handles the full spectrum of Houston-area pest threats.

Termite Treatment & Inspection

Baiting systems, liquid barriers, and pre-construction protection. We find termite activity before it becomes a costly structural repair.

Mosquito & Fire Ant Control

Yard barrier treatments reduce mosquito populations by up to 90%. Fire ant mound treatments and broadcast granules protect your lawn and family.

Roach & Ant Elimination

German and American cockroach programs with gel baiting and crack-and-crevice treatment. Interior ant trails eliminated at the colony source.

Rodent Exclusion

Entry-point sealing, snap traps, and bait stations. Rodents don't just cause damage — they carry disease. We stop them before they settle in.

General Pest Control

Spiders, silverfish, earwigs, stink bugs — quarterly and bi-monthly maintenance programs keep your home pest-free across all four seasons.

Ongoing Maintenance Plans

Monthly, bi-monthly, and quarterly service agreements — we schedule visits proactively so pests never get the chance to establish a presence in your home.
