What We Do

Full-Service Pest & Termite Solutions

From wood-destroying insects to general household pests — Big Dave's handles it all with the thoroughness that earned us 113 five-star reviews.

Termite Control

Liquid soil treatments, bait systems, and annual inspection programs. We treat the infestation and monitor your home to prevent future colonies from establishing.

Cockroach Treatment

Targeted gel baiting and crack-and-crevice treatments for German and American roaches. We follow up to make sure every harborage site is fully eliminated.

Mosquito Control

Barrier spray treatments targeting resting and breeding areas. Monthly and bi-monthly plans keep Houston's mosquito season from ruining your outdoor time.

Rodent Control

We seal exterior entry points, set up tamper-resistant bait stations, and remove existing populations — a complete rodent solution from the outside in.

Ant & Fire Ant

Interior ant trails and fire ant mounds both require different approaches. We identify the species and apply the correct treatment to eliminate colonies, not just scouts.

Quarterly Maintenance

Year-round protection with regularly scheduled visits. We treat seasonally relevant pests before infestations develop — preventative care that saves you money long-term.

Our Process

How Big Dave's Gets Results

A clear three-step process — no guesswork, no callbacks wondering what was done.

01

Thorough Inspection

We walk the entire property — attic to foundation — identifying active pests, entry points, harborage sites, and conditions attracting future infestations before touching a single product.

02

Precision Treatment

Armed with an accurate diagnosis, we apply the right treatment — not a generic spray. Whether it's termite bait stations, gel baits, exclusion foam, or barrier spray, we match the method to the pest.

03

Confirm & Maintain

We follow up to verify elimination, seal any remaining vulnerabilities, and set you up with a maintenance plan so pests don't return once the problem is solved.
