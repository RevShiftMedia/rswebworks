What We Fix

Full-Service Plumbing
for McKinney Homes

Every job is done right the first time. No callbacks, no upsells, no surprises. That's 19 years of the same promise.

Drain Cleaning

Slow drain or full blockage driving you crazy? We clear grease build-up, tree roots, and debris from kitchen, bathroom, and main sewer lines — fast, clean, and guaranteed.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water on a cold McKinney morning? We repair and replace tank and tankless water heaters — same-day service available for most brands and models.

Pipe Repair & Replacement

Burst pipe, corroded copper, or aging galvanized lines? We locate, diagnose, and repair pipe damage — from a single joint to a full repipe — with minimal drywall disruption.

Leak Detection

Hearing water you can't see? Our non-invasive leak detection technology finds slab leaks, hidden wall leaks, and under-slab breaks before they destroy your foundation or flooring.

Sewer Line Services

Sewage backing up into your home? We handle camera inspections, hydrojetting, trenchless sewer repair, and full line replacement — with upfront pricing before any work begins.

Emergency Plumbing

Plumbing emergencies don't wait for business hours. James Plumbing is on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including holidays. Call now, we answer.
