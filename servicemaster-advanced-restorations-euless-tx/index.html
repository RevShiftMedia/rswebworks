Restoration Services

Advanced Restoration — All Damage Types

ServiceMaster Advanced Restorations handles the full spectrum of property damage restoration for Euless and DFW homeowners and businesses — emergency response through complete rebuild.

Water Damage Restoration

Emergency extraction, structural drying, moisture mapping, and complete build-back from burst pipes, flooding, appliance failures, and storm intrusion.

Fire & Smoke Restoration

Structural repair, smoke and soot elimination, deodorization, and full reconstruction from minor kitchen fires to major structural events.

Mold Remediation

Certified assessment, containment, removal, and post-remediation clearance testing. We meet IICRC S520 standards and provide full documentation for insurance.

Contents Pack-Out

Careful removal, cataloging, off-site restoration, and return of salvageable belongings — furniture, electronics, documents, and valuables handled with care.

Storm Damage Restoration

Tarping, boarding, debris removal, and structural repair after hail, wind, and tornado events — fast response to prevent secondary damage from exposure.

Commercial Restoration

Large-loss commercial response with dedicated project managers, 24/7 crews, and documentation designed to satisfy insurance adjusters and minimize downtime.
