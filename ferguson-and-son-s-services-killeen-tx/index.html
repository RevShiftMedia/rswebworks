What We Handle

Plumbing Services
Killeen Relies On

From a dripping faucet to a burst pipe at 2am — we've seen it all and we fix it right. One call to a real family team.

Leak Detection & Repair

Slab leaks, hidden pipe leaks, and yard line breaks. We find it fast and fix it right — no guesswork, no unnecessary demo.

Drain Cleaning

Slow or completely backed-up drains — kitchen, bathroom, or main line. We clear it properly so it doesn't come back next month.

Water Heater Services

Replacement, installation, and repair on traditional tank and tankless water heaters. We stock the most common units to get your hot water back same day.

Pipe Repair & Replacement

Aging galvanized pipes, corroded lines, and freeze damage. We repipe sections or full homes and leave the job site clean.

Toilet & Fixture Repair

Running toilets, faucet replacements, shower valves, and garbage disposals. Small fixes done right the first time — no parts-store guesswork.

New Construction & Remodels

Rough-in and finish plumbing for room additions, kitchen and bath remodels. We work with your contractor or manage the whole project.
