Accepted Materials

What We Collect

We safely accept a wide range of household hazardous materials — keeping toxins out of landfills and protecting Denton's water supply.

🎨

Paint & Coatings

Latex and oil-based paints, stains, varnish, lacquer, sealers, and primers accepted in any size container.

⚗️

Household Chemicals

Cleaners, pesticides, herbicides, fertilizers, pool chemicals, and other hazardous solvents safely removed.

💻

Electronics & E-Waste

TVs, computers, monitors, phones, tablets, and other electronic devices responsibly recycled and diverted.

🔋

Batteries

Rechargeable, lithium-ion, lead-acid, and button cell batteries accepted in all sizes and types.

🛢️

Motor Oil & Fluids

Used motor oil, antifreeze, transmission fluid, brake fluid, and all types of automotive chemicals.

💡

Fluorescent Bulbs

CFL bulbs, fluorescent tubes, mercury-containing lamps, and UV bulbs disposed of safely and properly.

Simple Process

How It Works

Dropping off your household hazardous waste is quick and easy. Here's what to expect when you visit our facility.

1

Gather Your Items

Collect paint, chemicals, electronics, and other accepted materials from your garage, basement, or storage areas.

2

Drive to Our Facility

Head to 1527 S Mayhill Rd, Suite 300, Denton TX 76208. No appointment needed — just drive right in.

3

Drive-Through Drop-Off

Our staff will unload your vehicle in the drive-through lane. The entire process typically takes just minutes.

4

Certified Disposal

Your materials are sorted and sent to certified partners for proper processing, recycling, or safe destruction.
