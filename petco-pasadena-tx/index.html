What We Offer

Your Complete Pet Care Destination

Six departments under one roof — everything from daily nutrition to professional grooming and veterinary care for all your pets.

Pet Food & Nutrition
Premium Brands

Hundreds of premium dry, wet, and raw food options for dogs, cats, birds, and small animals. Our associates can help you find the right nutrition plan for your pet's age and health needs.

Grooming & Spa
Book an Appointment

Full-service grooming salon with baths, haircuts, nail trims, ear cleaning, and teeth brushing. Our certified groomers treat every pet with patience and care — from puppies to seniors.

Veterinary Services
Walk-Ins Welcome

VetCo Total Care services available in-store — vaccinations, wellness exams, parasite prevention, and more. Convenient, affordable vet care without a lengthy appointment wait.

Training Classes
All Skill Levels

Puppy classes, basic obedience, and advanced training led by experienced trainers. Build a stronger bond with your dog using positive reinforcement methods that actually work.

Aquatics & Fish
Live Fish In-Store

Freshwater and tropical fish, aquarium supplies, filtration systems, and expert setup advice. Whether you're starting your first tank or upgrading a reef setup — we have what you need.

Small Animals & Birds
Habitats & Supplies

Hamsters, guinea pigs, rabbits, birds — complete habitat setups, bedding, food, toys, and enrichment products for every small pet species we carry and more.
