Our Process

Identify. Target. Eliminate.

Every Alliance job follows the same three-step protocol — because shortcuts produce temporary results.

1
Precision Inspection
We identify the exact species, map the infestation zone, and document entry points before recommending any product. Correct ID = correct treatment.
2
Targeted Treatment
Species-matched products applied to harborage zones, entry points, and activity corridors. We go where the pests are, not just where they're visible.
3
Prevention & Guarantee
Exclusion recommendations, a written service report, and our full satisfaction guarantee. If pests return before the next scheduled service, we come back at no charge.
