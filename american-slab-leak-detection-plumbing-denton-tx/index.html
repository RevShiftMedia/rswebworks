What We Do

Slab Leak & Plumbing Services

Specialized detection and repair services for Denton homeowners. We handle everything from the first electronic scan to the final pressure test.

Slab Leak Detection

Electronic acoustic and thermal imaging technology locates hidden leaks under your foundation without invasive demolition.

Slab Leak Repair

Targeted pipe repair or full re-route using the smallest possible access point. We restore your floor and pressure-test the fix.

Pipe Re-routing

When a section of pipe is too damaged to repair in place, we re-route above the slab — clean, code-compliant, and long-lasting.

Water Line Services

Main water line diagnostics, repair, and replacement. We protect your home's primary supply from leaks that drive up water bills.

Leak Location Services

Already know you have a leak but don't know where? We offer standalone detection-only inspections with a full location report.

Emergency Plumbing

Active slab leaks cause foundation damage fast. Our emergency team is available 24/7 to respond before the damage spreads.
