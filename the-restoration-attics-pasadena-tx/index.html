How We Work

Inspect. Remove. Restore.

A complete three-phase process that leaves your attic cleaner than it was before the wildlife moved in.

1
Full Attic Inspection

We inspect the entire attic, crawlspace, roofline, and exterior — identifying every animal, every entry point, all contamination, and any structural damage. No corners skipped.

2
Removal & Exclusion

All wildlife removed humanely and in compliance with Texas regulations. Every entry point sealed with commercial-grade materials — metal mesh, caulk, and hardware cloth that animals cannot breach.

3
Full Restoration

Contaminated insulation removed, attic sanitized with enzyme-based cleaner, fresh insulation installed, structural damage repaired, pest treatment completed. Your attic is returned to you better than before.
