What We Handle

Full-Service Pest Protection for Mansfield Homes

Whether it's a one-time emergency or ongoing quarterly protection, Preston Pest Service delivers results that keep your family comfortable all year long.

General Pest Control

Comprehensive interior and exterior treatment for roaches, ants, silverfish, earwigs, and the full spectrum of common household pests plaguing Mansfield homes.

Termite Inspection & Treatment

Pre-purchase inspections, annual monitoring, and liquid barrier or bait station programs. We provide clear, detailed reports and coordinate with sellers when damage is found.

Rodent Removal & Exclusion

Live removal, snap traps, and full-property exclusion — we seal every gap, crack, and entry point so rodents never find their way back into your home.

Mosquito Barrier Program

Seasonal mosquito barrier applications to your yard, landscaping, and resting areas. Reduce mosquito populations by up to 90% so your family can enjoy Mansfield's outdoor spaces.

Bed Bug Elimination

Targeted heat and chemical treatments that reach every crack, seam, and hiding spot. We follow up until every bed bug and egg is confirmed gone — no exceptions.

Fire Ant Control

Fire ants are a year-round hazard in Mansfield yards. Our broadcast and mound treatments eliminate colonies fast and our prevention plans keep them from re-establishing.
