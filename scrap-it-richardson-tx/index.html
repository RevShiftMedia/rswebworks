What We Take

Accepted Scrap Materials

We accept a wide range of ferrous, non-ferrous, and specialty metals — plus appliances and electronic scrap from Richardson homes and businesses.

🔩

Ferrous Metals

Steel beams, iron pipes, sheet metal, rebar, and all forms of ferrous scrap picked up and recycled at fair market prices.

🪙

Copper & Wire

Bare copper wire, insulated wire, copper pipe, tubing, and fittings — some of the highest-value scrap we purchase.

🥫

Aluminum & Brass

Aluminum cans, gutters, siding, radiators, and brass fixtures — non-ferrous metals we weigh and pay by the pound.

🧺

Appliance Removal

Washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves, water heaters, and HVAC units hauled and recycled — appliances often have zero charge or net payout.

🚗

Auto Parts & Catalytic Converters

Rims, rotors, alternators, starters, catalytic converters, and other automotive scrap accepted at competitive rates.

🖥️

Electronic Scrap

Circuit boards, computer towers, server racks, and other e-scrap containing recoverable metals processed responsibly.

Simple Process

How Scrap-It Works

Getting cash for your scrap metal has never been easier. We handle everything from pickup to payment in four simple steps.

1

Call or Book Online

Describe your scrap materials and location. We'll give you an estimate and schedule your pickup.

2

We Come to You

Our crew arrives on schedule with the right equipment to safely load all scrap from your location.

3

Weigh & Price

Materials are weighed on certified scales. You see exactly what you have and get a transparent breakdown.

4

Get Paid

Receive payment on the spot for your metals. We haul everything away and leave your property clean.
