What D'Bug Man Does

Pest Solutions for Every Problem

Whether it's a single roach or a full-on rodent situation, D'Bug Man comes prepared, treats it right, and makes sure it stays gone.

Roach Control

Comprehensive German and American cockroach elimination with targeted baiting and barrier treatments that wipe out infestations at the source.

Rodent Removal

Full rodent inspection, entry-point sealing, trap placement, and follow-up to make sure mice and rats are gone for good — not just temporarily scared off.

Termite Inspection

Thorough inspections to catch termite activity before it causes serious damage. Early detection means lower repair costs and peace of mind.

Ant & Fire Ant Control

Targeted ant elimination with colony-killing treatments — including fire ant mounds that can make your yard unsafe for kids and pets.

Bed Bug Treatment

Heat and chemical treatments designed to eliminate bed bugs at every life stage — eggs, nymphs, and adults — so they don't come back.

Mosquito Barrier Spray

Seasonal perimeter treatments that knock down mosquito populations and keep your backyard comfortable all season long.
