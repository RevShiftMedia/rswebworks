Our Services

Lawn Care & Pest Control — All Year

From spring green-up to fall aeration, from fire ant control to termite monitoring — Custom Turf handles every outdoor need your Colleyville home has.

Lawn Fertilization Programs

Customized seasonal feeding programs matched to your grass type and soil conditions — Bermuda, St. Augustine, Zoysia, or Fescue — delivering thick, dark-green results.

Weed Control

Pre-emergent and post-emergent treatments that keep dandelions, crabgrass, and broadleaf weeds out of your turf — applied at the right time to actually work.

Lawn Aeration & Overseeding

Core aeration breaks up compaction and improves root depth. Paired with overseeding, it thickens thin lawns and fills bare spots before the growing season.

Mosquito & Fire Ant Control

Barrier spray programs that knock out mosquitoes at the source and targeted granular treatments that eliminate fire ant mounds — fast and thoroughly.

Termite Treatment

Liquid soil barrier and bait station programs that protect your home's foundation from subterranean termites — the number one structural pest in North Texas.

General Pest Control

Interior and exterior treatment for ants, roaches, spiders, and seasonal pests — quarterly or bi-monthly programs to keep your home protected year-round.
