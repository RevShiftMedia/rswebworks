Expert Plumbing for McKinney Homes & Businesses

Every service is performed by licensed, insured professionals — with technical precision and code-compliant workmanship on every call.

Drain Cleaning

Recurring clogs and slow drains signal a system problem, not just surface buildup. We use professional hydro-jetting and cabling equipment to fully restore drainage — and diagnose whether a deeper blockage is developing.

Get Expert Service

Water Heater Repair

A water heater that fails is more than an inconvenience — it disrupts your family's daily routine. We diagnose tank and tankless units with precision, advise you on repair versus replacement with full cost transparency, and restore hot water fast.

Get Expert Service

Pipe Repair

Hidden pipe damage can saturate insulation and framing long before visible water appears. We locate breaks with minimal disruption, select the right material — copper, PEX, CPVC — and make a clean, code-compliant repair built to last.

Get Expert Service

Leak Detection

Elevated water bills and damp spots on walls often point to a pressurized slab or concealed wall leak. We deploy professional acoustic and thermal imaging to pinpoint the source, then present a clear repair plan with no unnecessary excavation.

Get Expert Service

Sewer Line Service

A compromised sewer main is a public health risk that affects your entire home. We provide full camera inspections, hydro-jetting, and trenchless or traditional line repair — with permits, inspections, and full code compliance handled end to end.

Get Expert Service

Emergency Plumbing

A burst main or sewage backup demands an expert response — not a rushed patch. Our emergency team arrives with the tools and training to make a proper, lasting repair under pressure, not just a temporary stop-gap until a follow-up visit.
