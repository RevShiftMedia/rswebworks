Pest Management Services

Comprehensive Coverage for Every Pest

Versacor's managed programs cover the full spectrum of pests common to Southlake and Tarrant County homes — with scheduled visits and documented service records.

General Pest Management

Ants, roaches, spiders, earwigs, and seasonal invaders handled on a scheduled basis — so problems are caught and addressed before they escalate.

Rodent Exclusion & Control

Full structural exclusion combined with trapping and monitoring. We eliminate the current population and seal the pathways they're using to enter.

Termite Monitoring

Bait station networks placed around your foundation create a perimeter that detects and eliminates termite colonies before they reach your structure.

Mosquito & Tick Programs

Monthly or bi-monthly outdoor barrier treatments from spring through fall. Reclaim your yard for family gatherings, cookouts, and everyday outdoor living.

Bed Bug Treatment

Heat and chemical combination protocols that reach every life stage. Our technicians confirm elimination before closing out the service — no guesswork.

Commercial Pest Management

Documented, compliant pest programs for restaurants, offices, and retail — built to satisfy health inspectors and protect your business reputation.
